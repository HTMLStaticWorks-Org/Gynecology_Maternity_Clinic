Clinical Biography

A Calm, Steady Presence From First Scan to First Cry

Dr. Amara Okafor trained in Obstetrics and Gynecology at Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust in London before completing her advanced fellowship in minimal-access gynecologic surgery and maternal medicine. Over 17 years, she has personally guided more than 2,100 births—earning a reputation for calm, unhurried counsel and one of the region's highest successful Vaginal Birth After Cesarean (VBAC) rates.

Her clinical focus bridges physiological birth and modern obstetric safety, offering women clear evidence, emotional support, and unhurried appointments. Whether managing complex pregnancies, supporting water birth delivery, or performing delicate fertility-sparing laparoscopy, Dr. Okafor advocates for your autonomy at every milestone.

Care Philosophy

Core Pillars of Patient Practice

Physiological & Gentle Birth

Evidence-Based VBAC Advocacy

Fertility-Sparing Keyhole Surgery

Expert laparoscopic excision of endometriosis and uterine fibroids designed to preserve future fertility.
