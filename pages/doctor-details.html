Clinical Biography

A Calm, Steady Presence From First Scan to First Cry

Dr. Amara Okafor trained in Obstetrics and Gynecology at Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust in London before completing her advanced fellowship in minimal-access gynecologic surgery and maternal medicine. Over 17 years, she has personally guided more than 2,100 births—earning a reputation for calm, unhurried counsel and one of the region's highest successful Vaginal Birth After Cesarean (VBAC) rates.
