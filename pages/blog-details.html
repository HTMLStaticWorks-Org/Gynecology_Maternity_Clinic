Your First Trimester: What to Expect, Week by Week

By Dr. Clara Sterling, MD, FRCOG • Medically Reviewed November 2026

1. Weeks 1 to 6: The Quiet, Invisible Miracle

Even before a home test shows two lines, your body is orchestrating an extraordinary vascular and endocrine transformation. Human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) and progesterone rise rapidly to nourish the uterine lining, while your blood volume begins a 45% expansion. During these early weeks, taking 400 to 600 mcg of methylated folate daily supports healthy neural-tube closure.

2. Weeks 7 to 10: Your First Viability & Dating Scan

At Bloom & Cradle, we invite families for their first unhurried viability scan between weeks 7 and 8. Seeing the rhythmic flicker of your baby's heartbeat (typically 140 to 170 beats per minute) provides profound reassurance and establishes an accurate estimated due date.

3. Easing First-Trimester Nausea & Fatigue

First-trimester exhaustion is physiological—building a placenta requires immense metabolic energy. To soothe morning queasiness, keep protein-rich crackers or almonds beside your bed before rising, sip cold ginger-lemon infusion in small amounts between meals, and ask your obstetrician about vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) regimens.

4. Weeks 11 to 13: Nuchal Translucency & NIPT

As you approach the close of the first trimester, the placenta assumes full hormone production—often lifting the veil of early nausea. Between weeks 11 and 13+6, we perform your high-resolution Nuchal Translucency (NT) ultrasound alongside optional cell-free DNA (NIPT) blood screening.
