Fetal Medicine & Diagnostic Imaging

High-Definition 4D Ultrasound & Fetal Doppler Suite

Crystal-clear anatomical reassurance, placental blood-flow assessment, and an unforgettable cinema-screen bonding moment for your family.

Clinical Overview

Precision Maternal-Fetal Imaging in a Warm Sanctuary

Our Fetal Medicine Suite combines Voluson E10 4D acoustic beamformer imaging with pre-warmed organic aloe gel, a 65-inch family viewing wall, and real-time explanation from a consultant subspecialist. Every scan evaluates both structural anatomy and uteroplacental circulation.

Indications

Who This Service Is Designed For

Recommended across all three trimesters: Weeks 7–10 (Early Viability & Dating), Weeks 11–13+6 (Nuchal Translucency & Early Anatomy), Weeks 19–22 (Detailed Structural Anomaly & Fetal Echo), and Weeks 28–36 (4D Growth, Biophysical Profile & Umbilical/Middle Cerebral Artery Doppler).

What Happens During Your Scan

4-Step Scan Experience

Step 01

History & Gestational Review

Your consultant reviews prior scans, blood work, and any specific questions before dimming the suite lights.

Step 02

2D Biometry & Organ Survey

Systematic head-to-toe anatomical measurement of brain structures, four-chamber heart, spine, kidneys, and limbs.

Step 03

Color Doppler & 4D Bonding

Evaluation of placental cord flow followed by lifelike 3D/4D surface rendering of your baby's expressions.

Step 04

Immediate Report & Portal Clips

Receive your signed diagnostic report immediately, plus HD stills and video clips uploaded to your Patient Portal.

Before & After

Preparation & Post-Scan Comfort

Preparation: For scans under 14 weeks, drink 2 glasses of water 45 minutes prior so your bladder is comfortably full. For 20-week+ anomaly or 4D scans, a emptied bladder is fine, and a light snack 20 minutes before often encourages gentle fetal movement. Wear two-piece attire for effortless access.
Recovery: Non-invasive with zero downtime—you may resume normal daily activities immediately.

ALARA Safety Principle

Safety Notes & Acoustic Standards

Diagnostic obstetric ultrasound uses non-ionizing sound waves—never radiation. Our specialists strictly adhere to ISUOG and AIUM ALARA ("As Low As Reasonably Achievable") thermal and mechanical index guidelines. While high-resolution ultrasound detects the vast majority of structural variations, no prenatal scan can guarantee detection of 100% of genetic or late-onset conditions.
