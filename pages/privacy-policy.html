1. Collection of Protected Health Information (PHI)

Bloom & Cradle Women's Health & Maternity Clinic collects personal and clinical data strictly necessary to provide safe obstetric, gynecologic, reproductive, and neonatal care. This includes your contact details, medical history, ultrasound imaging, laboratory assays, and birth preferences.

2. How We Use & Safeguard Your Records

Your health information is accessed exclusively by your authorized clinical care team (obstetricians, midwives, neonatologists, and diagnostic specialists) and, only where authorized by you, your designated health insurer for direct billing verification. We never sell, rent, or monetize patient data.

3. Encryption & Patient Portal Security

All Patient Portal communications, 4D ultrasound clips, and diagnostic PDFs are protected using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Role-based access controls and audit logging protect your records against unauthorized viewing.

4. Your Patient Rights & Data Portability

You have the right to inspect, download, amend, or request a complete portable archive of your medical records at any time through the Settings tab of your Patient Portal or by contacting our Medical Records Desk.

5. Contact Our Data Privacy Officer

For questions regarding medical confidentiality or data protection, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@bloomandcradle.example or write to Suite 300, 420 Aurelia Boulevard.
