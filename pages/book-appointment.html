What to Bring to Your Appointment

To make your visit effortless, please bring or upload the following items to your Patient Portal beforehand:

1. Photo ID & Insurance Card

For seamless cashless verification at our private check-in lounge.

2. Prior Scans & Lab Reports

Recent blood panels, hormone tests, or prior ultrasound records.

3. Current Medications & Supplements

Including prenatal vitamins, iron, thyroid medication, or herbal blends.

4. Your Questions & Birth Partner

Partners or a trusted support person are warmly welcomed at every visit.
