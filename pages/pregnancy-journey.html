Clinical Screening Calendar

Essential Prenatal Milestones Timeline

Never miss a key scan, blood panel, or immunization window.

Weeks 7 – 13

Booking, NIPT & Nuchal Scan

Confirmation of heartbeat & due date at week 8, optional NIPT chromosomal screen at week 10, and nuchal translucency scan at week 12.

Weeks 19 – 26

20-Week Anomaly & Glucose Screen

High-definition structural organ & heart survey at week 20, followed by gestational glucose tolerance and ferritin testing at week 26.

Weeks 28 – 38

Growth Doppler, Tdap & Birth Plan

Tdap whooping cough vaccine at week 28, 4D growth & placental Doppler at week 32, GBS swab at week 36, and weekly birth-readiness visits.

Key Nutrients for Mother & Baby

Prenatal Movement

Pelvic Mobility & Diaphragmatic Breathing

Aim for 150 minutes weekly of low-impact swimming, brisk walking, and prenatal yoga. In the third trimester, focus on pelvic floor relaxation ("down-training") and hip-opening postures.

24/7 Clinical Vigilance

When to Call Our 24/7 Triage Immediately

Never hesitate or wait until morning if you notice any of the following symptoms:

  • • Noticeable reduction in your baby's usual movements after 24 weeks
  • • Any fresh vaginal bleeding or sudden gush/trickle of clear fluid
  • • Severe persistent headache, visual spots, or sudden facial/hand swelling
  • • Regular painful contractions before 37 weeks
  • • Fever above 38°C (100.4°F) or pain under the right ribs
