1. Medical Educational Disclaimer

All articles, calculators (including the due-date calculator, kick counter, and weight-gain tool), and week-by-week pregnancy guides on this website are provided for educational purposes only and do not establish a physician-patient relationship or substitute for in-person clinical evaluation.

2. Outpatient Appointment Rescheduling & Cancellations

Because we reserve 30 to 60 minutes exclusively for each consultation, we kindly request 24 hours' notice if you need to reschedule or cancel an outpatient appointment. You may reschedule effortlessly with zero fee inside your Patient Portal.

3. Maternity Package Reservations & Refunds

Maternity package deposits ($500) hold your private birthing suite allocation around your estimated due date. Should your clinical pathway change or should you relocate prior to 34 weeks of gestation, your deposit is refundable less any completed outpatient visits.

4. Patient Portal Security & Acceptable Use

You are responsible for keeping your Patient Portal credentials confidential. Portal messaging is designed for non-urgent clinical follow-up and routine questions.

5. Urgent & Active Labour Situations

Never use website forms or portal messages for urgent symptoms such as reduced fetal movement, vaginal bleeding, or regular labour contractions. Always call our 24/7 Labour & Maternal Triage Desk directly at +1 (800) 555-0199.
