How We Work

Coordinated, Trauma-Informed Clinical Methodology

Every consultation at Bloom & Cradle follows our three-pillar clinical charter: Listen Deeply, Diagnose Precisely, Co-Design Respectfully. You remain in control of every examination, pace, and treatment decision.

Same-Visit Imaging

Pelvic or prenatal ultrasound is performed in the same suite by your consultant—no separate radiology referral trips.

Pain-Free Protocols

Warmed instruments, topical numbing or paracervical block for IUD/biopsy procedures, and patient-controlled stop signals.

Shared Decision Making

Transparent Care Plans in Your Portal

Every visit concludes with a clear written summary sent directly to your Patient Dashboard.
