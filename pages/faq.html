FAQ Categories

Category 01 • Appointments & First Visits

Scheduling & Consultations

No referral is required for self-pay or PPO insurance patients. You may book directly with any of our OB-GYN consultants, fetal medicine specialists, or midwives.

Category 02 • Pregnancy & Prenatal Scans

Antenatal Care & Diagnostics

We recommend booking as soon as you have a positive home test. Your first viability and dating scan is typically scheduled between 7 and 8 weeks of gestation.

Category 03 • Labour, Delivery & Birthing Suites

Giving Birth at Bloom & Cradle

Yes. Continuity of care is central to our model. Your named lead obstetrician and primary midwife team oversee your pregnancy and attend your delivery.

Yes! Every suite includes a full partner daybed, organic meals for your partner, and open collaboration with your certified birth doula.

Category 04 • Billing, Packages & Insurance

Financial & Coverage Questions
